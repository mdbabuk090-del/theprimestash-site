Editorial Policy

Every story is reviewed before it is published. We do not publish content involving real identifiable private individuals, graphic violence, sexual content, or hate speech. Where a story is inspired by a real situation, names and details are changed.

What we will not publish

  • Content about real, identifiable private individuals presented as fact.
  • Graphic violence, sexual content, or content involving minors in a sexual context.
  • Hate speech or content that demeans a protected group.
  • Medical, legal or financial advice presented as professional guidance.

Corrections

If we publish something inaccurate or misleading, we correct it and note the correction on the page.

Advertising and independence

Advertising is sold and served by third parties. Advertisers have no influence over the stories we publish or how they are written.

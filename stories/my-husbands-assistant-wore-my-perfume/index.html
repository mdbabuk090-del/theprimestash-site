My Husband's Assistant Wore My Perfume. I Said Nothing for Six Months.

At a company dinner, Elise recognizes her custom perfume on her husband's assistant—a scent made in only two bottles a year. She says nothing, goes home, and discovers the second bottle is missing on the same day a suspicious company transfer was approved.

Chapter 01 — The Scent at Dinner

Chapter 1 of 10

Elise Marchetti smelled the perfume before she saw who was wearing it.

She stopped with one hand on the back of her chair.

Across the private dining room, Grant was laughing at something his assistant had said.

Sloane Whitaker turned.

The scent reached Elise again.

Rosewood.

Bitter orange.

A trace of smoke that appeared only after the perfume warmed against skin.

Elise knew every note because she had worn it for fifteen years.

There were two bottles made each year.

No shelf stock.

No samples.

No gift sets.

Her name was engraved on the glass.

“Everything okay?” Grant asked.

Elise sat down.

“Perfect.”

Sloane smiled. “I love your earrings.”

“Thank you.”

Grant lifted his wine.

“To the best quarter we’ve had in three years.”

The executives at the far end of the table raised their glasses. Elise did too.

She did not look at Sloane again until dessert.

Grant talked about expansion.

Sloane took notes even though dinner was supposed to be informal.

Elise watched the way Grant leaned toward her when she spoke.

Not touching.

Nothing obvious.

Nothing useful.

At home, Grant went upstairs first.

Elise stood in the dark kitchen and opened the cabinet above the refrigerator.

A cream-colored box sat behind a stack of serving platters.

She took it down.

Inside was the bottle she had opened in January.

Half full.

Under it was the second compartment.

Empty.

Elise stared at the velvet indentation.

The second bottle should have been there.

Unopened.

She remembered the invoice arriving in December.

Two bottles.

Paid from her personal account.

Delivered to the house.

She closed the box.

Grant called from upstairs.

“You coming?”

“In a minute.”

She carried the box into her office and locked the door.

Her laptop opened to the company email system.

Elise created a new folder.

She named it MISC 2026.

Then she searched Grant’s calendar.

Sloane’s name appeared everywhere.

Flights.

Client dinners.

Late strategy sessions.

Nothing unusual for an assistant.

Nothing unusual at all.

That was the problem.

Elise did not want a suspicion.

She wanted a timeline.

At 11:18 p.m., Grant knocked once and opened the door.

“You’re still working?”

“Quarter close.”

He leaned against the frame.

“You work too much.”

“So do you.”

He smiled.

For one second, Elise wondered how many times she had seen that smile and believed it meant exactly what it appeared to mean.

“You were quiet tonight,” he said.

“Tired.”

“Sloane thinks you don’t like her.”

Elise looked up.

“Did she say that?”

“No. I can tell.”

Interesting.

Elise closed her laptop.

“I barely know her.”

“She’s good at her job.”

“I’m sure she is.”

Grant stepped inside and kissed the top of Elise’s head.

The smell of her perfume was faint on his jacket.

Not enough for proof.

Enough for sleep to become impossible.

After he left, Elise opened the laptop again.

She searched household deliveries from December.

The perfume shipment had arrived on December 14 at 2:06 p.m.

Signed for by G. Marchetti.

Grant.

She saved the confirmation to MISC 2026.

Then she opened the company’s online banking portal.

She had spent twenty years learning that betrayal became expensive when it touched paperwork.

The operating account looked normal.

Payroll looked normal.

Vendor payments looked normal.

Then she saw three transfers to a consulting company she did not recognize.

Each was just under the threshold requiring her secondary approval.

Elise opened the first payment.

Requested by Grant.

Approved by Sloane.

She stared at the timestamp.

December 14.

The same day the second bottle disappeared.

Synopsis

Elise Marchetti does not confront her husband when she discovers his affair. She opens a folder instead. Over six months, a stolen perfume bottle leads her from quiet infidelity to shell-company transfers, falsified wire approvals, and a personal guarantee designed to leave the losses in her name. When Grant's assistant finally brings proof, Elise must decide whether revenge means destroying them both—or exposing exactly who engineered the betrayal.

Main characters

Elise MarchettiA finance executive who refuses to make a scene before the paperwork is ready.
Grant MarchettiHer charismatic husband, who believes every consequence can be postponed until someone else pays it.
Sloane WhitakerGrant's assistant, whose affair with him leaves her holding both stolen perfume and dangerous financial evidence.
Teresa MarchettiGrant's mother and controlling shareholder, who loves order enough to turn on her own son when the numbers prove he lied.

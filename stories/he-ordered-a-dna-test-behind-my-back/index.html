Family Secret · 10 chapters

He Ordered a DNA Test Behind My Back. The Lab Called Me First.

A lab calls Maren about DNA results she never ordered. Her husband secretly tested their nineteen-year-old daughter—and included his mother in a three-generation family panel that was supposed to settle one question but exposes a much older one.

Chapter 01 — The Lab Called

Chapter 1 of 10

“The results are ready.”

Maren Sutcliffe stopped halfway through fastening her bracelet.

“I’m sorry?”

The woman on the phone repeated herself.

“The family relationship panel. I’m calling to confirm the mailing address before we release the sealed report.”

Maren looked at the bedroom door.

Peter was downstairs making coffee.

Their nineteen-year-old daughter had left for campus an hour ago.

“I didn’t order a family relationship panel.”

A pause.

“Are you Maren Sutcliffe?”

“Yes.”

“And the account ending in 4408 is yours?”

“Yes.”

“Then I need to verify one more thing before we continue.”

Maren sat on the edge of the bed.

“What exactly was tested?”

The woman’s tone changed.

More careful now.

“I’m Dr. Adaeze Okoro, the lab director. The order was placed through your family account. It includes three samples.”

“Whose?”

“I can’t disclose names until I verify the account holder.”

Maren gave her date of birth.

Billing zip code.

The last four digits of the card on file.

Dr. Okoro exhaled softly.

“The samples are listed as your husband, your daughter, and Camille Sutcliffe.”

Maren’s hand went cold.

Peter.

Their daughter.

His mother.

Not Maren.

“What kind of test?”

“A three-generation kinship panel.”

“Why?”

“That’s not something the lab determines.”

Maren stood.

The floor felt strangely uneven beneath her feet.

“Did Peter order this?”

“The order was submitted under his name.”

She closed her eyes.

For eighteen years, she had been the woman who married into the Sutcliffe family.

Camille never said Maren did not belong.

She did not have to.

It lived in small things.

The family silver kept in Camille’s house.

The way holiday photographs were arranged.

The way Camille still called the house Peter’s home when Maren had paid half the mortgage for eight years.

But this was different.

This was not a look across a dinner table.

This was DNA.

“When was it ordered?”

“Three weeks ago.”

Three weeks.

Peter had kissed her that morning.

They had watched a movie Friday night.

He had asked her opinion on a new car.

All while waiting to see whether the daughter they raised together belonged to him.

Maren’s throat tightened.

“What did the results say?”

Another pause.

“I’m not comfortable discussing the report by phone.”

“Why not?”

“There is a chain-of-custody issue attached to one sample. I’m legally required to note it before release.”

Maren gripped the edge of the dresser.

“What issue?”

“One sample was logged under a family-relationship kit that does not match the relationship declared on the order.”

“I don’t understand.”

“I know.”

Dr. Okoro’s voice softened.

“I’m sending the report sealed. Do not open it until I call you again.”

Maren almost laughed.

“You’re telling me a lab has my husband’s DNA, my daughter’s DNA, and my mother-in-law’s DNA, but I shouldn’t open the results?”

“I’m telling you the paperwork and the biological relationships do not align cleanly. I want to verify the sample log first.”

Downstairs, Peter called.

“Maren? Coffee’s getting cold.”

She stared at the bedroom door.

Dr. Okoro said, “Mrs. Sutcliffe?”

“Yes.”

“I’ll call you within forty-eight hours.”

The line disconnected.

Maren walked downstairs.

Peter smiled when he saw her.

“You look pale.”

She set the phone on the kitchen counter.

“Did you have our daughter’s DNA tested?”

The smile disappeared.

Peter’s hand froze around his coffee cup.

For three seconds, he said nothing.

Then he looked away.

“Who called you?”

That was answer enough.

Synopsis

Maren Sutcliffe expects a secret DNA test to prove her husband doubted her. Instead, the results confirm their daughter is his—and reveal that Peter is not biologically related to the mother who has controlled their family for thirty-six years. With a bloodline inheritance clause about to activate, Maren realizes the test was never just about paternity. It was Camille's attempt to protect an old lie before anyone thought to test upward.

Main characters

Maren SutcliffeA woman married into a family obsessed with bloodlines, who refuses to let a secret test define what family means.
Peter SutcliffeMaren’s husband, kind in person but too used to letting his mother make decisions he should have questioned himself.
Camille SutcliffePeter’s controlling mother, who built thirty-six years of family order around a biological secret she was terrified to expose.
Dr. Adaeze OkoroThe lab director whose insistence on chain-of-custody accuracy turns a paternity test into the collapse of a family myth.

About Us

We publish short emotional family dramas — the kind of story you finish in one sitting and then think about for the rest of the day. Every story here is written to be read on a phone, chapter by chapter.

What we publish

Short emotional fiction: family drama, second chances, quiet justice. Each story is broken into short chapters so it reads comfortably on a phone.

Who writes them

Our stories are written and edited in-house. Every chapter is reviewed before publication against the editorial standards described on our Editorial Policy page.
